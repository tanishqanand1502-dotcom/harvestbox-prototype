Questions people ask first

What happens if I do not choose my dishes?

We remind you twice on WhatsApp. If we do not hear back, we send your usual box with dishes picked from the ones you have rated well.

When am I charged?

Only when the week's order locks, 24 hours before delivery. A skipped or paused week is never charged, so there are no refunds to wait for.

I changed my mind after the order locked. What now?

The ingredients are already bought, so the box cannot be cancelled. You can move the delivery slot or send the box to a friend. Once a month we waive a late skip.

I used to be a customer. Has anything changed?

Yes. There is no lock-in, skipping is free, and you can change your box until the day before. Sign in with your old number to pick up where you left off.

Can I order just one dinner?

The smallest box is two dinners. For a single meal tonight, look for HarvestBox kits on quick-delivery apps in your city.
